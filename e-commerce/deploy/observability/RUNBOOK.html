E-commerce platform RUNBOOK.md

Runbook#

One section per alert (platform.rules.yml, and the burn-rate alerts generated by generate.py). Each says what the customer sees, how to confirm, and what to do. Dashboards are in Grafana's E-commerce folder.

Error budget burn#

ErrorBudgetBurnFast (page) and ErrorBudgetBurnSlow (ticket), label slo.

  • Customer impact: availability SLOs: requests failing with 5xx; latency SLOs: pages or checkout slower than the budget in SPEC §2.2 N2.
  • Confirm: SLOs and error budgets → the SLO's burn-rate panel. Then Services: rate, errors, duration for the service behind the route; in Explore → Tempo, { resource.service.name = "api-gateway" && status = error } or { duration > 1s } for example traces.
  • Act: a deploy in the last hour → roll it back first. A dependency down (database, Kafka, payment provider) → see its own alert. Overload → check the HPA is scaling and the gateway's admission control is shedding load as designed.

Outbox backlog stale#

OutboxBacklogStale (page), label service: the oldest unpublished event is older than 30 s.

  • Impact: other services see stale data: carts, stock, search and emails lag behind orders and products.
  • Confirm: Checkout, events and Kafka → Transactional outboxes. The service's log has Outbox relay pass failed, retrying in … ms with the Kafka error.
  • Act: Kafka unavailable or rejecting writes → fix Kafka (broker logs, disk, under-replicated partitions). The relay retries with backoff up to 30 s and publishes what Kafka accepts, so it catches up by itself once Kafka is healthy.

Topic growing faster than its events#

TopicGrowingFasterThanItsEvents (page), label topic: the topic grows more than ten times faster than its outbox publishes.

  • Impact: none at first (consumers deduplicate by event id), then consumer lag, a full disk and stale read models.
  • History: in November 2026, before the relay was fixed, a relay that saw one partition fail re-sent its whole batch every 100 ms: 444 events became 30 million messages in two hours.
  • Confirm: Messages produced per second by topic against Domain events published per minute.
  • Act: find the producer (the topic's owning service), look for repeated send failures in its log, fix the cause. Consider lowering the topic's retention temporarily to reclaim disk.

Dead-letter queue#

DeadLetterQueueNotEmpty (ticket), label topic: events failed five times and were parked in <topic>.dlq.

  • Impact: the effect of those events is missing (a cart not cleared, a review not allowed, an email not sent).
  • Confirm: dlq_replay.py list --dlq <topic>.dlq (dlq-replay) shows each event's key, the group that parked it and the reason; the traceparent header leads to the trace, the consumer's log has the exception.
  • Act: fix the cause, then dlq_replay.py replay --dlq <topic>.dlq (try --dry-run first). Replayed events are deleted from the DLQ, so the alert resolves; consumers are idempotent, so replaying is safe.

Consumer lag#

ConsumerLagGrowing (ticket), labels consumergroup, topic.

  • Impact: read models go stale (search results, carts after an order, reviews).
  • Act: KEDA scales consumers on this lag up to the partition count; if it is at the maximum, look for a slow dependency (OpenSearch, Cassandra, Redis) on the consumer's dashboard.

Service down#

ServiceDown (page), labels service, instance: Prometheus cannot scrape it for 2 minutes.

  • Act: kubectl -n e-commerce-shop describe pod and the previous container's logs (OOM kills, failed probes). Locally this also fires for services whose compose profile is not running.

Payment outcome unknown#

PaymentOutcomeUnknown (ticket): checkouts that timed out waiting for the payment provider.

  • Impact: those orders were cancelled; customers were told they will not be charged, and the reconciler refunds any charge that went through.
  • Act: payment-service latency and the provider's status page; if the provider is degraded, consider the checkout admission rate.

Payment decline rate high#

PaymentDeclineRateHigh (ticket): more than 30 % of checkouts declined for 15 minutes.

  • Act: a provider incident, a broken payment form, or card testing. For card testing, look at declines per customer and per address and tighten the gateway's checkout limits.

Rate limiter degraded#

RateLimiterDegraded (ticket): the gateway cannot reach Redis and limits per instance.

  • Act: restore redis-ratelimit. Until then each gateway replica applies the full limit, so the effective limit is multiplied by the replica count.

PostgreSQL backups#

PostgresBackupTooOld (ticket): a cluster's last base backup is more than 26 hours old. PostgresWalArchivingStalled (page): no write-ahead log file archived for 15 minutes.

  • Confirm: kubectl -n e-commerce-platform get backups (the latest <cluster>-nightly and its error); kubectl cnpg status <cluster> (WAL archiving, last failed WAL); the plugin-barman-cloud container's log in the primary pod.
  • Act: usually object storage: expired or wrong credentials (backup-storage secret), the bucket missing or full, the egress gateway blocking storage.shop.example. Fix it; archiving resumes by itself and the next backup runs at 02:00, or take one now with kubectl cnpg backup <cluster> --method plugin --plugin-name barman-cloud.cloudnative-pg.io. While archiving is stalled, watch the cluster's disk: WAL is kept until it is archived.
  • Restore: 15.5.
